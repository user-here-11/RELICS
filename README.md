# Relics — Abandoned but not Forgotten

**Live Website:** https://lambent-druid-29b5c5.netlify.app  
**Created by:** Gazzl Razmy

## About the Project
**Relics** is an interactive storytelling web experience built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge **"Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars."** 

Every time humanity reaches for another world, machines get left behind. Relics brings together the stories, mission timelines, landing coordinates, and final transmissions of 13 historic NASA spacecraft across the Moon, Mars, and Deep Space—complete with an interactive landing-site map and a 15-question trivia quiz hosted by three guides (Moonling, Marsling, and Deepling).

## Key Features
* **Interactive Stories:** Detailed chronological profiles for 13 missions across the Moon (Surveyor 3, Apollo 11, 12, 15), Mars (Viking 1, Pathfinder & Sojourner, Spirit & Opportunity, InSight, Ingenuity), and Deep Space (Pioneer 10, NEAR Shoemaker, Dawn, Kepler).
* **Landing Site Map:** Interactive Moon and Mars map plotting exact published NASA latitude and longitude coordinates for each relic.
* **Three-World Quiz:** A 15-question interactive quiz divided into Moon, Mars, and Deep Space rounds, sharing verified NASA facts after every question.

## Why It Matters & What's Next
* **Sparking Curiosity:** Turns intimidating space archives into an exciting adventure for kids, students, and families.
* **Honoring Space Heritage:** Reminds explorers that the rovers and probes across the Moon, Mars, and deep space are living monuments to discovery.
* **Future Expansion:** Plans to add more historic spacecraft, real NASA rover audio clips, and extra quiz levels.
